THE PROBLEM

Customers don’t see your silos.

They see one brand, and expect one connected experience.

Inside most organisations, CX influence is split across teams, channels, systems and priorities.

This lack of singular ownership and misalignment creates fragmented journeys, inconsistent delivery, duplicated effort and poor performance.

Node exposes the gaps your customers are already feeling.
